How It Works

From check-in to consultation in three simple steps.

1

Report Symptoms

Select what you are experiencing, rate the severity, and indicate any red flags.

2

Get Your Score

The algorithm computes a priority score and inserts you into the sorted queue. You receive a ticket number.

3

Track Your Position

Watch your live queue position. Staff calls patients in priority order from their dashboard.
